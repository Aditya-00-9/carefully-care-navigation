Privacy, plainly

Your words belong to you.

Prototype privacy notice · Last updated October 6, 2026. Carefully is an early demonstration, not a clinical service. This notice explains what this prototype does with text you enter.

By default, your notes stay in this page

The prototype does not create an account, store your symptom text in a database, or add analytics or advertising trackers. The browser holds the text while this page is open. It clears when you leave or reload the page.

Voice recognition

When you tap the microphone, speech recognition is provided by your browser and the browser’s speech provider. Its handling of audio and transcripts depends on that provider; Carefully cannot promise that audio stays on your device. You can type instead.

Optional AI translation

Only when you check the optional AI box and request a summary, the text and selected language are sent to our serverless endpoint, then to Cerebras Inference for an English visit-note summary. The API key stays server-side. Cerebras processes that request under its privacy policy. You can skip AI and use the local, typed flow. Please do not include names, addresses, phone numbers, or other identifying details.

Safety and health information

This prototype does not diagnose, estimate disease probabilities, provide treatment advice, or connect you with verified clinicians. Its emergency phrase check is incomplete and may miss danger or incorrectly match a phrase. If you may be having an emergency, contact local emergency services now. Do not use this page to decide whether it is safe to wait.

Demo limitations

This is not a complete legal privacy notice for a production health product. Before serving patients, the product owner must identify the responsible organization, jurisdiction, vendors, retention and support practices; assess applicable health-data and medical-device rules; and obtain professional privacy, security, and clinical review. Do not enter a real patient’s identifying or sensitive health details in this public demonstration.
